Real Talk

What bad work really looks like

Most homeowners and property managers don't know what poor workmanship looks like until it fails. We see it constantly — and fix it right the second time.

Tile & Flooring

Cracked Floor Tile Is Not Normal Wear

Cracked tile is almost always the result of inadequate mortar coverage during installation. Tile is extremely durable when set correctly — cracks mean the installer didn't know what proper coverage requires, or skipped the prep entirely. Most homeowners assume it's just how tile ages. It isn't. It's a workmanship failure that will keep spreading.

Exterior & Grounds

The Leaning Mailbox Post Problem

A mailbox post that leans within a year of installation wasn't set properly to begin with — wrong depth, wrong concrete mix, or no concrete at all. Small jobs done wrong become bigger problems. We install things once, correctly, so you're not calling someone again in six months.

Hidden Damage

The Previous Contractor's Hidden Surprise

We open walls and regularly find work that should never have been done that way. Improper wiring, pipes assembled with the wrong materials, water intrusion from improper substrate choices behind tile. We document everything we find and give you options before we proceed — every time, without exception.

Pricing & Process

How our pricing works

We believe in transparent, honest pricing. Here's exactly what to expect from first contact to job completion — no surprises, no runaround.

1

You Tell Us What's Going On

Fill out our quick form or call/text directly. Photos help us give you a more accurate range — sometimes we can ballpark over the phone without a visit for straightforward jobs like outlet replacements, faucet swaps, or basic repairs where the scope is clear.

2

We Give You a Honest Ballpark

For most jobs we provide a three-number range: a low end (best case, no surprises), a likely middle (typical for this type of job), and a high end (if complications arise). We tell you exactly what would push each number. For simple, well-defined jobs — outlet replacement, switch swap, basic hardware — we can often give you a firm flat price upfront.

3

Complex Jobs Get a Paid Assessment

For jobs where a proper estimate requires us to open walls, investigate, or scope a multi-phase project, we charge a $75 assessment fee applied toward the job if you move forward. This protects both of us — you get an accurate number, we get paid for our expertise and time.

4

You Authorize Every Scope Change

If we discover unexpected conditions mid-job — a previous contractor's improper work, concealed damage, non-standard materials — we stop, document it, and present you with options before we touch anything. You decide how to proceed. No surprise charges. Ever.

A note on scope changes: Properties have histories we can't see until we open walls or pull fixtures. When unexpected conditions arise, we consider this a separate authorization — not a surprise charge. Clients who understand construction appreciate this transparency. It's how professional work gets done right.
