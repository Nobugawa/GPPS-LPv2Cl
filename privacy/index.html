Who We Are

Great Precision Property Services ("GPPS," "we," "us," or "our") is a licensed and insured property repair company based in Riverdale, NJ, serving Morris, Passaic, and Bergen County. Our website is greatprecisionservices.com.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website forms, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your property address or zip code
  • Photos or descriptions of your repair issue
  • Your preferred contact method and timing

We collect this information solely to respond to your service inquiry and provide you with a quote.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Contact you regarding your service request
  • Provide estimates and schedule work
  • Send appointment reminders or follow-up communications related to your job

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing purposes.

SMS and Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and consent to text messaging, we may contact you via SMS regarding your service request, estimate, or appointment. Message and data rates may apply.

  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP
  • For help, reply HELP
  • Message frequency varies based on your inquiry
Your phone number and SMS consent will never be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, affiliate, or marketing organization. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all data sharing.

We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third parties. We do not share your information with affiliates or partners for marketing or lead generation purposes.

Third-Party Tools

We use the following third-party tools to operate our website and intake process. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Tally.so — form collection (tally.so/privacy)
  • Google Analytics — anonymous website traffic analysis (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Netlify — website hosting (netlify.com/privacy)

These tools may collect standard technical data such as IP addresses and browser types for operational purposes. None of these tools receive your personal contact information for marketing purposes from us.

Data Retention

We retain your inquiry information only as long as necessary to complete your service relationship with us or as required by applicable law.
